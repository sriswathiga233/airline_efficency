Passenger Load Factor measures how much of the available seating capacity is occupied, while Aircraft Utilisation measures the proportion of total operational time spent flying.

Final Conclusion

      

        
Overall, the dataset shows an average passenger load factor of 81.6% and aircraft utilisation of 73.6%. While route-level occupancy is generally above the 70% review threshold, several aircraft-route combinations show substantially lower efficiency. The analysis suggests that aircraft assignment and operational utilisation should be considered alongside route demand when planning fleet deployment.
